## Prototypes

### Proto 1 — `index.html`
Vertical scroll navigation. Fixed pill in top-left shows current section name + scroll progress ring. Clicking the pill opens a 3×3 card menu overlay to jump to any section.

### Proto 2 — `prototype-2.html`
Zoom wall navigation. Full-screen 7-column × 3-row grid of cards. Drag left/right to pan. Click any card to zoom it into full screen. × button zooms back out to the wall.
- Starts centered on the 3 core columns
- 2 brand foundation columns on the left (A1–A6)
- 3 core columns in the center (01–09)
- 2 application columns on the right (B1–B6)
